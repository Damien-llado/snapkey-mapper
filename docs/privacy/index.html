Privacy

What this website and the app record, and what they do not. Last updated on 17 November 2026.

The app

SnapKey Mapper collects nothing: no account, no tracker, no ads, no crash reporting. Your shortcuts, the key pattern and the last 20 presses stay on your phone. The source code is public on GitHub.

Audience of the website

The website measures its audience with Microsoft Clarity (clicks, scrolling, recordings of visits), only if you accept in the banner. If you refuse, Clarity is not loaded and sets no cookies. You can change your mind at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Download links

The download buttons and links (/download, /get/…) note each click on our side, to know where downloads come from: the site or page you came from, the campaign tag of the link, your browser, country and network. No IP address and no cookie. Where you arrived from is kept in your browser for the current tab only (session storage), to add it to the download link. The file itself is served by GitHub.

Other services

The pages ask api.github.com for the star and download counts, which sends your IP address to GitHub, and keep the numbers for an hour in your browser. The setup page embeds a YouTube video from youtube-nocookie.com, which loads when the page opens. The website is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes each request to serve it.
