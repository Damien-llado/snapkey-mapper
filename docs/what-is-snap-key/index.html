What is the Snap Key on an OPPO phone?

The Snap Key is a physical button on the upper left side of some OPPO phones, such as the Find X9 series. On ColorOS it launches one action from a short, fixed list, for example AI Mind Space or the flashlight. SnapKey Mapper, a free app, lets it open any app or link instead.

By default

What the Snap Key does out of the box

On ColorOS 16 the Snap Key can only do what OPPO lists: AI Mind Space, camera, flashlight, recorder, translate, screenshot, sound mode or do not disturb. You pick one of them in the phone settings. There is no option to open an app of your choice.

That is the gap SnapKey Mapper fills. It keeps the key, and adds what the settings leave out.

Phones

Which phones have a Snap Key

The Snap Key is the side key of OPPO's recent phones. The Reno 16 series calls it the AI Snap Key. OnePlus has a similar key on some models, called the Plus Key. SnapKey Mapper works with the keys below.

Under the hood

How an app can use a key that ColorOS locks

Every press of the Snap Key leaves a line in the system log, KEYCODE_ACTION_BUTTON_CLICK. SnapKey Mapper reads the log, spots that line and opens the app or link you chose. It needs one permission, READ_LOGS, that Android lets you grant once without root. No accessibility service, and the app collects nothing.

The safety section of the home page lists what the app can and cannot do.

FAQ

Questions about the Snap Key

What is the Snap Key in an OPPO phone?

A physical button on the upper left side of some OPPO phones. In the settings you choose one action for it from a fixed list. With SnapKey Mapper it opens any app or link, and each of single, double, triple and long press can open something different.

Is the Snap Key the same as the OnePlus Plus Key?

They are similar side keys on phones of the same group. SnapKey Mapper works with both: see the OnePlus Plus Key page.

What is the AI Snap Key of the Reno 16?

It is the name the OPPO Reno 16, Reno 16 Pro and Reno 16 F give to their side key. The app works with it. If yours does not respond, tell us on GitHub.

Can the Snap Key open any app?

Not with OPPO's settings alone, which offer a fixed list of actions. With SnapKey Mapper, yes: any installed app or any link.

Is it related to other products called Snap Key?

No. The name is also used for unrelated things, such as keyboard features and game settings. This page is only about the side key of OPPO phones.
