OPPO Find X9 Pro · ColorOS 16 · No root

Customize the Snap Key of the OPPO Find X9 Pro

The Snap Key is the button on the upper left side of the Find X9 Pro. ColorOS lets it do one action from a fixed list. SnapKey Mapper, a free and open source app, makes it open any app or link, with a different one on single, double, triple and long press.

What ColorOS lets the Snap Key do

On the Find X9 Pro, ColorOS 16 offers a fixed list for the Snap Key: AI Mind Space, camera, flashlight, recorder, translate, screenshot, sound mode or do not disturb. You pick one of them in Settings › Snap Key. There is no option to open an app of your choice.

What the app adds

One Snap Key, four shortcuts

Each gesture opens its own target. Set one, two, three or all four.

  • Four gestures

    Single, double, triple and long press (half a second). A series of presses waits a fraction of a second only when a longer series is set, to tell them apart; otherwise it fires at once.

  • Any app or link

    Pick an installed app, paste a link, or write an intent: link for anything Android can do. The key opens it, with no root.

  • Built-in actions

    Flashlight on or off, ring then vibrate then silent, play or pause, next and previous track. Nothing opens: the action just happens. Silent needs Do Not Disturb access, which you allow once.

  • A shortcut library

    Ready-made shortcuts grouped by app, with a field for each value and a Test button. See the library.

Setup

Set it up on the Find X9 Pro

The short version. The full guide has every screen and the fixes for each error.

  1. Set OPPO's own Snap Key action to nothing

    In Settings › Snap Key, set the key to do nothing, so it no longer triggers AI Mind Space or the flashlight. ColorOS still logs every press, so SnapKey Mapper keeps working.

  2. Install the APK and allow restricted settings

    Download the APK and open it. Then Settings › Apps › SnapKey Mapper, tap ⋮ then Allow restricted settings: Android asks for it with any app installed outside the Play Store.

  3. Let the app grant itself the log permission

    The app needs one permission, READ_LOGS, that Android lets you grant once without root. On ColorOS it stays blocked until the hidden switch Disable system optimisation is on, and that switch only shows when the phone is in English. The full edition then sets the permission through the phone's own wireless debugging: Wi-Fi is needed this once, with no computer. Step 3 of the full guide has each screen.

  4. Allow display over other apps and battery

    In SnapKey Mapper, tap Allow display over other apps and Battery: do not restrict. On ColorOS, also set the app battery usage to No restrictions and lock SnapKey Mapper in the recent apps.

  5. Choose what each gesture opens

    Pick an app, a link or a built-in action for single, double, triple and long press, and tap Test. The app then listens by itself.

FAQ

Questions about the Find X9 Pro Snap Key

Where is the Snap Key on the Find X9 Pro?

On the upper left side. The Quick Button, on the right edge, is a camera control and has not been tested with SnapKey Mapper.

Can the Snap Key open any app on the Find X9 Pro?

Not with OPPO's settings alone: ColorOS offers a fixed list of actions. With SnapKey Mapper, yes: any installed app or any link, and a different one on each of single, double, triple and long press.

Can I turn the flashlight on with the Snap Key?

Yes. The built-in action Flashlight on or off turns it on, or off if it is on, on the gesture you choose. No app opens.

Can the Snap Key switch between ring, vibrate and silent?

Yes. The built-in action Ring, vibrate, silent moves to the next mode on each press. Silent needs the Do Not Disturb access, which Android asks you to give once; without it, vibrate goes back to ring.

Do I need root or a computer?

No. The full edition grants itself its one permission, READ_LOGS, through the phone's wireless debugging, with Wi-Fi this once. No root, no Shizuku, no accessibility service. The lite edition has no internet permission and is set up from a computer.

The Snap Key stopped working after a reboot

Tap the "SnapKey Mapper is off" notification, then "Allow one-time access". Also check that battery usage is set to No restrictions and that the app is locked in the recent apps.
