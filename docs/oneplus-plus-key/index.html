Remap the OnePlus Plus Key to open any app

The Plus Key of a OnePlus phone offers a short, fixed list of actions: Mind Space, the flashlight, a ring mode. SnapKey Mapper, a free and open source app, lets you customize it: any app or link on a single, double, triple or long press.

The key

What OnePlus lets the Plus Key do

On several OnePlus models the Plus Key takes the place of the alert slider. In the phone settings you pick one action for it. Owners on r/oneplus name the choices they use: Mind Space (the OnePlus AI), flashlight, camera, screenshot, voice recorder, translate, do not disturb, and a ring, vibrate and silent switch. The exact list depends on the model and the software version.

What the settings do not offer is the app you actually want. Owners keep asking for their own assistant, a custom camera app, or a different action on a double or triple tap.

What the app adds

One Plus Key, four shortcuts

Each gesture opens its own target. Set one, two, three or all four.

  • Four gestures

    Single, double, triple and long press (half a second). A series of presses waits a fraction of a second only when a longer series is set, to tell them apart; otherwise it fires at once.

  • Any app or link

    Pick an installed app, paste a link, or write an intent: link for anything Android can do. The key opens it, with no root.

  • Built-in actions

    Flashlight on or off, ring then vibrate then silent, play or pause, next and previous track. Nothing opens: the action just happens. Silent needs Do Not Disturb access, which you allow once.

  • Voice recorder

    A shortcut opens the phone's voice recorder, ready for a voice note.

  • A shortcut library

    Ready-made shortcuts grouped by app, with a field for each value and a Test button. See the library.

Setup

Set it up on a OnePlus

The short version. The full guide has every screen and the fixes for each error.

  1. Free the key from its own action

    In the phone settings, open the Plus Key setting. If there is a do nothing choice, pick it, so the key stops opening Mind Space or the flashlight at the same time. On OPPO phones the system still logs every press, so SnapKey Mapper keeps working; tell us on GitHub if your OnePlus behaves differently.

  2. Install the APK and allow restricted settings

    Download the APK and open it. Then Settings › Apps › SnapKey Mapper, tap ⋮ then Allow restricted settings: Android asks for it with any app installed outside the Play Store.

  3. Let the app grant itself the log permission

    The app needs one permission, READ_LOGS, that Android lets you grant once without root. The full edition does it through your phone's own wireless debugging: Wi-Fi is needed this once, with no computer. Screens can differ on OxygenOS, and some builds need a hidden switch first: step 3 of the full guide explains it.

  4. Choose what each gesture opens

    Pick an app, a link or a built-in action for single, double, triple and long press, and tap Test. The app then listens by itself.

FAQ

Questions about the Plus Key

Can I remap the OnePlus Plus Key to open any app?

Not with OnePlus's settings alone: they offer a fixed list of actions. With SnapKey Mapper, yes: any installed app or any link, and a different one on each of single, double, triple and long press.

Can the Plus Key do a double tap or a triple tap?

Yes. Single, double, triple and long press each have their own target, an app, a link or a built-in action. To clear a gesture, pick it in the app and tap Remove.

Can I use the Plus Key as an alert slider?

Yes. The built-in action Ring, vibrate, silent moves to the next mode on each press. Silent needs the Do Not Disturb access, which Android asks you to give once; without it, vibrate goes back to ring. The difference with the phone's own setting is that you choose the gesture, and keep the others for other things.

Can I put the flashlight on the Plus Key?

Yes. The built-in action Flashlight on or off turns it on, or off if it is on. No app opens.

Can it take a screenshot, toggle Do Not Disturb or start Circle to Search?

Not as built-in actions. The built-in actions are the flashlight, ring, vibrate and silent, play or pause, next track and previous track. Anything that opens from an app or a link works too, but a screenshot or a Do Not Disturb switch does not.

Do I need root, Shizuku or Tasker?

No. The app needs one permission, READ_LOGS, that it grants itself once through your phone's wireless debugging. No root, no Shizuku, no Tasker and no accessibility service. The main FAQ explains what the app can and cannot see.

Which OnePlus phones have a Plus Key?

The app works with the Plus Key of the OnePlus 15, 15R, 13s, 13T and Nord 5. The 13s and the 13T carry it. If yours has a Plus Key and is not listed, try it and tell us on GitHub.

It does not work on my OnePlus. What now?

Open Doesn't work on my phone? in the app: it restarts the listening, restores the original key pattern if it was changed, and prepares a GitHub issue with your model, system and version. You can also send us the log line of your key, from the key finder of the app.
